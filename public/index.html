Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about our AC and DC EV charging infrastructure, installation and support across India.

What does DP Power Solutions do?

DP Power Solutions Pvt Ltd designs, manufactures and installs EV charging infrastructure across India, including AC chargers, DC fast chargers and end-to-end charging solutions for residential, commercial and industrial customers.

What is the difference between an AC charger and a DC fast charger?

An AC charger (7kW-22kW) converts power inside the vehicle and is best for slower overnight charging at homes and offices. A DC fast charger (120kW-1200kW) converts power externally and delivers rapid charging in minutes, making it ideal for commercial stations, fleets and highways.

Which EV charging solutions does DP Power Solutions offer?

Our range includes the AIR 5 Smart AC Charger (7kW/11kW/22kW) and the DOER series of DC fast chargers from 120kW up to 1200kW, covering residential, commercial and highway charging needs.

Do you provide EV charger installation services?

Yes. We provide complete EV charger installation services, including site assessment, electrical load planning, installation, commissioning and after-sales support.

Do you install EV chargers for commercial properties and businesses?

Yes, we work with commercial property owners, offices, hotels, fleet operators and highway developers to design and install scalable EV charging infrastructure suited to their traffic and power requirements.

Can I install an EV charger at my home or apartment?

Yes. Our AIR 5 Smart AC Charger is designed for homes and apartment complexes, with 7kW, 11kW and 22kW output options to match your electrical connection and daily charging needs.

What charging speeds do your DC fast chargers support?

Our DOER DC fast charger range spans 120kW, 240kW, 480kW and up to 1200kW, with dual or multi-gun CCS2 outputs and dynamic load balancing across connected vehicles.

Are your EV chargers OCPP compliant?

Yes, our AC and DC chargers support the OCPP 1.6J protocol, allowing them to connect to third-party charge point management systems for remote monitoring and control.

What connectivity options do DP Power Solutions chargers support?

Depending on the model, our chargers support WiFi, Bluetooth, 4G and Ethernet connectivity for remote monitoring, diagnostics and OCPP-based management.

How do users authenticate and pay at your charging stations?

Our chargers support RFID card, NFC and QR code based authentication, enabling secure access control and integration with charge point management and payment systems.

Are your EV chargers weatherproof for outdoor installation?

Yes. Our chargers are built with IP54/IP65 and IK10-rated enclosures, making them weatherproof and impact-resistant for outdoor and public installations across Indian climates.

Do you offer maintenance and AMC support for installed chargers?

Yes, we offer preventive maintenance, remote monitoring and annual maintenance contracts (AMC) to keep your EV charging infrastructure running reliably.

How long does it take to install a commercial EV charging station?

Installation timelines depend on site readiness and electrical capacity, but typically range from a few days for a single AC charger to a few weeks for a multi-gun DC fast charging station, after site survey and approvals.

Which cities and regions does DP Power Solutions serve?

We are headquartered in Mumbai, Maharashtra and actively serve Mumbai, Thane and the wider Maharashtra region, with EV charging infrastructure projects across India.

Can DP Power Solutions design charging infrastructure for electric bus depots and fleets?

Yes. Our DOER 480kW and Ultra Fast (up to 1200kW) distributed chargers are designed for electric bus depots, logistics fleets, airports and highway charging corridors.

Are there government subsidies for installing EV chargers in India?

Several central and state schemes offer incentives for EV charging infrastructure in India, though availability and eligibility vary by state and category. Contact our team for current guidance relevant to your project.

What is dynamic load balancing and why does it matter?

Dynamic load balancing automatically distributes available power across multiple connected vehicles in real time, maximizing charging efficiency without exceeding the site's electrical capacity.

Is DP Power Solutions hardware made in India?

Yes, DP Power Solutions focuses on cost-effective, Made-in-India EV charging infrastructure engineered for reliability under Indian grid and climate conditions.

What ongoing support do I get after installation?

Every installation includes commissioning support, and customers can opt into remote monitoring and AMC plans for continued 24/7 support and rapid fault resolution.
